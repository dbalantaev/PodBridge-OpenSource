# Completion report template

## Outcome

State what now works or which artifact is ready in one paragraph.

## Changed

- `path:Symbol` — what changed and why.

## Verified

- `exact command` — outcome.

## Limitations and residual risk

- What was not verified, why, and the practical impact.

Do not list internal exploration steps unless they help someone verify the result. Do not call a check successful unless its command ran and completed successfully.
