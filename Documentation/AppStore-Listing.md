## English (primary UI language)

### Promotional text

Move the music you own from Files on your iPhone to a supported click-wheel iPod.

### Description

PodBridge helps you manage music on a supported click-wheel iPod directly from your iPhone. Choose a folder in Files, connect your iPod, review the detected tracks, and transfer them to the iPod music library.

Features:

- Import music and M3U playlists from Files.
- Review tracks before transferring, including their title, artist, album, duration, bitrate and embedded artwork.
- Add music to the native iPod library, menus and Cover Flow.
- See the selected iPod model, capacity and available space.
- Browse your iPod library and use Library Tools to find duplicate tracks, incomplete metadata and missing artwork.
- Back up the iPod library before a transfer and verify the resulting database.

PodBridge supports iOS 17 and later. It is designed for supported iPod Classic, iPod Video and iPod nano models running Apple's original firmware. The iPod Classic 160 GB (Late 2009 / 7th generation) is the hardware profile tested for this release; other supported profiles are experimental.

Use PodBridge only with music you are authorized to use. The app does not distribute music files and is not affiliated with Apple. Back up your iPod and begin with a small transfer before moving a large library.
